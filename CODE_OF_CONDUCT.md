# Code of Conduct

Pilot follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Expected:** empathy and kindness; respecting differing opinions and experience; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; focusing on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable:** sexualised language or imagery; trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without permission; anything reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@paylo.one**. All complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy is respected.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviours they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.
